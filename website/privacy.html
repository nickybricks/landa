Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026

Landa turns your voice into text. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what happens to your audio and your data.

The short version

  • Landa only records between the moment you start and stop with the shortcut. It is never listening in the background.
  • By default, your audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription and is not stored after the text is returned.
  • You can switch to a fully local transcription model — then your audio never leaves your computer.
  • Your settings live on your own device. We do not run accounts and we do not have a user database.

Microphone & audio

Your microphone is only accessed while a recording is active. You press the shortcut to start, speak, and press it again to stop — recording ends immediately. Audio is captured solely to produce a transcription and is discarded once that text has been delivered to your active app.

Transcription

Landa supports two transcription modes:

  • Cloud (default): audio is sent over an encrypted connection to OpenAI's Whisper API. OpenAI processes it to return text and, per their API policy, does not use API audio to train their models. The audio is not retained by Landa.
  • Local: a transcription model runs entirely on your machine. In this mode no audio or text ever leaves your device.

AI text profiles

If you enable a profile that cleans up or reformats your dictation, the transcribed text (not audio) is sent to a language-model provider to produce the polished version. This only happens when a profile is active. With profiles off, no text is sent anywhere.

Settings & storage

Your configuration — shortcut, language, selected model, API keys you enter — is stored locally in your operating system's standard application-data folder. It is never uploaded to us.

This website

This site uses privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Vercel Analytics) to count page views. No personal profiles are built and no cross-site tracking is performed.

Your rights

Because Landa does not maintain accounts or a central store of personal data, there is no profile for us to export or delete. If you have any question about your data, contact us and we'll respond.
